Made for the scene

Less hunting. More shooting.

SnapMap brings the useful pieces of automotive photography and local car culture into one place without turning it into another noisy social app.

⌖

Find photo spots

Discover garages, downtown angles, overlooks, murals, backroads, industrial locations, and other community-shared places worth shooting.

◉

Discover events

Find nearby car meets, shows, cruises, and gatherings with dates, locations, directions, and community updates.

▣

Share your work

Post photos from spots and events, credit photographers, build a creator profile, and connect with people who actually care about the same scene.

★

Weekly challenges

Get a fresh photo theme each week, submit your shot, browse community entries, and earn a featured winner spot.

Who it's for

Cars and photography first.

Automotive photographers, enthusiasts, creators, and meet-goers are the core of SnapMap. But if you're chasing a great city view, scenic overlook, architecture spot, or hidden location, you're not left out. A good spot is a good spot.

α

SnapMap is currently in Alpha.

That's why SnapMap is a web app right now. Keeping it on the web lets us ship fixes, polish features, and respond to real feedback much faster while the app is still evolving. You may run into the occasional bug, unfinished edge, or feature that changes as we improve it.

Want it to feel like an app on iPhone? Open SnapMap in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen. You'll get a full-screen icon and app-like launch without downloading anything.
